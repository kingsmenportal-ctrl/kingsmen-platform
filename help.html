Help and safety

Answers before you take the next step

Use these checks to protect your information and avoid missed deadlines or fake opportunities.

Can I register a Kingsmen account here?

Not on this temporary public website. Account registration and secure candidate-specific features are not connected to these pages yet.

Can Kingsmen submit an application for me?

No. These pages link to official starting points. You must follow the provider’s instructions and submit through its official channel.

Does a link mean the opportunity is open?

No. A link is a starting point, not confirmation of an active intake, deadline, eligibility or outcome. Check the provider’s current official listing.

Can I upload my ID, CV or results here?

No. Do not upload sensitive documents to this public static site. Use a verified provider portal when required.

How do I avoid opportunity scams?

Verify the website and contact details independently, be cautious of guaranteed placement or approval claims, never share passwords or OTPs, and do not pay unofficial intermediaries.

What about privacy?

Do not enter personal or financial information into these pages. This static site does not offer a private Kingsmen account, personal document storage or candidate-specific tracking.

Need a specific starting point?

Use the searchable directory to filter resources by pathway, or open the application guide to prepare your next submission.
