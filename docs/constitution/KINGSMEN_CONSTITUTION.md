# KINGSMEN CONSTITUTION - N00 Doctrine Lock

## Non-negotiable laws

1. Oracle Free / Oracle Lite is the default deployment constraint.
2. PostgreSQL/backend/policies/founder approvals are the authority.
3. No production claim without proof.
4. No provider live claim without provider proof.
5. All candidates can see and be assessed across all six gates:
   - higher_education
   - tvet
   - seta_learnership
   - internship
   - apprenticeship
   - employment
6. Packages control service intensity, speed, quantity, monitoring depth, human review depth, SLA, escalation and support intensity. Packages do not hide gates.
7. CAO is one Kingsmen parent application. Programme choices inside CAO are child choices, not separate Kingsmen applications.
8. Submitted requires proof.
9. Outcome/success requires proof.
10. Public success story requires proof and consent.
11. Email is the primary communication nervous system.
12. WhatsApp is urgent-only.
13. SMS is backup-only.
14. Playwright is probe/scout only.
15. CAPTCHA/OTP bypass is forbidden.
16. Portal blockers create MAP/manual fallback.
17. Source confidence is required before final fee, deadline, payment-channel, or official instruction.
18. Candidate identity/document/data sharing with partners requires specific consent.
19. AI may suggest, summarize, rank and flag. AI must not govern money, refunds, official outcomes, submissions, fraud, privacy, hard blockers, pricing, package entitlements, or growth cap.
20. Growth cap starts at 500 active candidates. Cap limits volume, not capability.
21. Founder approval is required for high-risk changes, live activation, cap changes, production claims, pricing changes, and public success claims.
22. Evidence and audit records are append-first; corrections create new audit evidence.
23. Airtable is mirror only.
24. n8n is thin wrapper only.
25. PowerShell Creator materializes, validates, reports and deploys; it is not the business brain.

## Production boundary

N00 itself is not production readiness. It is the constitutional authority that later modules must load and validate.
