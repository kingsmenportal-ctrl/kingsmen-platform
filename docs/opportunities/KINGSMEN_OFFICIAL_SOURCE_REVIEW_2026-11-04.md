### Ekurhuleni 2027 Community Bursary

Official page: https://www.ekurhuleni.gov.za/campaigns/applications-for-the-2027-community-bursary-programme-now-open/  
Application portal: https://bursaries.ekurhuleni.gov.za  
Published 30 October 2026; closing 30 November 2026.

The source lists South African citizenship and permanent Ekurhuleni residence, age 18–35, Grade 12 completion, a 2027 acceptance/registration letter and annual household income not exceeding R600,000. It includes selected full-time undergraduate studies at accredited universities, universities of technology and TVET colleges, including specific technical/artisan qualifications. Certified ID, acceptance/registration, recent proof of residence and household-income evidence are listed. Map the programme-specific fields and TVET qualifications before candidate matching.

### Funza Lushaka 2027

Official Department article: https://www.education.gov.za/ArchivedDocuments/ArchivedArticles/2027FunzaLushakaBursaryProgramme.aspx  
Application portal: https://www.eservices.gov.za  
Applications opened 1 October 2026; first-time applicants close 31 January 2027; returning bursars close 30 November 2026.

The Department describes the programme as targeting youth below 30 with a matric Bachelor’s pass or equivalent university-entry eligibility. The source lists tuition, accommodation or transport, meals, learning materials and related support. This is not sufficient by itself to determine a candidate’s eligible teacher-education phase, subject, institution or priority discipline; review current programme rules before display.

### UCT Postgraduate Financial Aid Bursary 2027

Official page: https://uct.ac.za/students/fees-funding-postgraduate-degree-funding-bursaries-scholarships/uct-merit-and-need-awards  
Closing date: 10 November 2026.

The source lists Honours, Master’s and Doctoral study for South African citizens and permanent residents, all fields. First-year applicants must submit their 2027 UCT admissions application before the postgraduate funding application. Applications use the PeopleSoft Student Portal’s PG Online Funding Application. Late or incomplete applications are excluded. UCT says selection is holistic and points applicants to detailed funding guidelines; review those guidelines before candidate-facing matching.

### CPUT ABB Education Trust 2027

Official CPUT source: https://www.cput.ac.za/student/quick-links/bursary-opportunities  
Current CPUT page states applications opened 2 November 2026 at 08:00 and close 14 November 2026 at 16:00.

Published mandatory criteria include Black female South African citizens; completion of first year with a minimum 60% pass aggregate; annual household income below R600,000; no funding for repeat courses or outstanding balances. Preference is given to Electrical, Mechanical and Industrial Engineering degrees or higher diplomas. CPUT’s official page has an Online Application action, but the exact 2027 form URL was not captured in this review. Do not publish a guessed form link.

Official vacancy: https://thepresidency.gov.za/node/10523  
Published 26 October 2026; closing 6 November 2026. Centre: Pretoria. Salary: R932,292 per annum, Level 11.

The listed requirements are a Senior Certificate plus an appropriate IT Bachelor’s degree, National Diploma or equivalent NQF Level 6 qualification, and 3–5 years’ relevant systems-development experience according to SDLC. The advert requires a completed and signed Z83 and detailed CV only. Submit to applications@presidency.gov.za for the attention of Ms L Mphahlele, or hand deliver at Government Avenue, Union Buildings, Pretoria. The advert specifies 16:30 for walk-ins and midnight for email submissions on the closing date. Confirm the advert’s time reference and current vacancy status again immediately before candidate matching.
