## 2. What Number 13 means in plain Kingsmen terms

Playwright is a controlled browser scout.

It may support Kingsmen by opening pages, inspecting forms, checking whether portals load, identifying fields, taking screenshots where permitted, detecting login barriers, observing errors, and preparing a report for Jarvis and the Commander.

Playwright may help with:

- portal availability checks,
- form field discovery,
- page structure inspection,
- application-flow scouting,
- opening-date and closing-date portal checks,
- document-upload flow observation,
- fee-instruction page observation,
- screenshots for evidence,
- detecting portal downtime,
- detecting changed selectors,
- detecting CAPTCHA barriers,
- detecting OTP barriers,
- preparing Manual Application Package recommendations,
- proving that automation is blocked or unsafe.

But Playwright must never become:

- final truth,
- official result authority,
- payment authority,
- submission authority without approval,
- CAPTCHA bypass,
- OTP bypass,
- hidden automation,
- uncontrolled application submitter,
- candidate-data exposure route,
- replacement for human/operator review where required,
- replacement for PostgreSQL Truth Vault,
- replacement for evidence ledger,
- replacement for manual fallback.

Playwright’s correct place is after Jarvis routing and policy. It receives a bounded scout task, observes safely, returns evidence, and stops.

## 3. Difference between Number 12 and Number 13

Number 12 is OpenClaw gateway.

Number 13 is Playwright portal scout.

OpenClaw may route governed tasks to agents and tools.

Playwright may inspect browser-based portals and pages.

Examples:

- “Can OpenClaw route this task?” → Number 12.
- “Can Playwright check this portal?” → Number 13.
- “Can an agent decide this?” → Number 11.
- “Can Playwright submit the form?” → Number 13 with strict approval, evidence, and fallback rules.
- “Can Playwright bypass CAPTCHA?” → Number 13 with a clear no.
- “Write the Playwright script” → Number 10 after the Number 13 boundary is clear.

Playwright may be routed by OpenClaw in some designs, but its own role remains scout-only.

## 10. Policy-gate role

Playwright must be behind policy gate.

Policy checks:

- What portal is being scouted?
- Is the action read-only, observation-only, draft-only, or submission-capable?
- Does the task involve private candidate data, documents, payments, login, official applications, or external messages?
- Does CAPTCHA, OTP, login, or other access control appear?
- Does the task require Commander approval?
- Does the task require human/operator review?
- Will evidence return to Kingsmen?
- Is manual fallback available?
- Is there a safe stop condition?
- Is Playwright necessary, or is this a useless layer?

Rules:

- Playwright is scout-only by default.
- Playwright must not bypass CAPTCHA.
- Playwright must not bypass OTP.
- Playwright must not fake official status.
- Playwright must not mark payment status.
- Playwright must not force unsafe submissions.
- Playwright must not continue after blocker detection.
- Playwright must stop when the source is blocked, uncertain, or outside boundary.
- Manual fallback/MAP must be generated or recommended where automation is unsafe.
- Evidence must return to Jarvis and the evidence ledger.
