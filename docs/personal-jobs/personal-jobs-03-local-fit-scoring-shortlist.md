# PERSONAL-JOBS-03 — Local Fit Scoring / Shortlist Decision Desk

PERSONAL-JOBS-03 creates a local shortlist decision desk for job leads captured in PERSONAL-JOBS-02.

## Creates

- `/personal-job-desk/shortlist`
- Local fit scoring for external job leads.
- Shortlist bands:
  - Crown Target
  - Strong Route
  - Watchlist
  - Research First
- Recommended CV and motivation-letter pairing from `CV-01` to `CV-15` and `ML-01` to `ML-15`.
- Local shortlist export to JSON.
- Local-only status changes.

## Boundaries

This does not send applications, emails, WhatsApps, portal submissions, or payments. It does not claim production readiness or Oracle deployment.

## Why it matters

PERSONAL-JOBS-02 captures opportunity leads. PERSONAL-JOBS-03 turns those leads into a ranked action queue so the strongest opportunities can be prepared first.
