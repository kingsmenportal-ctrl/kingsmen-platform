# PERSONAL-JOBS-04 - CV & Motivation Letter Apply Pack Builder

PERSONAL-JOBS-04 creates a local draft-only apply pack builder for the personal job desk.

## Creates

- `/personal-job-desk/apply-pack`
- Reads PERSONAL-JOBS-03 shortlist output from localStorage.
- Generates local draft apply packs.
- Supports 15 CV directions and 15 matching motivation-letter directions.
- Provides template switching:
  - Executive Classic
  - ATS Plain
  - Retail & Service
  - Administrative Precision
  - Learnership Entry
  - General Worker
  - Digital Support
  - Hospitality & Care
- Produces role-specific CV direction.
- Produces role-specific motivation-letter draft.
- Produces ATS keyword guidance.
- Produces truth-safe warnings and preflight checklist.
- Exports local JSON.

## Hard boundaries

This module does not send applications, emails, WhatsApps, payment actions, portal submissions, or employer contact. It does not claim production readiness, Docker runtime, or Oracle deployment.

## Truth doctrine

PERSONAL-JOBS-04 must never invent experience, qualifications, certificates, licences, references, skills, employment dates, addresses, achievements or proof. It may prepare wording only from real profile data and opportunity context.
