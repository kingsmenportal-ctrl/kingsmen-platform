# BATCH 33 - Free Destiny Map Intake Capture Proof

## Purpose

BATCH 33 adds the smallest useful Free Destiny Map intake capture surface.

It gives candidates a public, mobile-friendly way to express their current stage, province, route interest, biggest blocker, and readiness goal.

## Scope

Local candidate UI only.

## What this proves

- `/free-destiny-map/` has a visible intake form surface.
- The form asks only non-sensitive route-discovery questions.
- The form does not submit to a backend.
- The form does not store candidate data.
- The form does not collect contact details.
- The form does not collect ID numbers.
- The form does not process payments.
- The form does not send email, WhatsApp, or SMS.
- The form does not claim production readiness.
- The form does not claim Oracle deployment.

## Hard boundary

This batch is not production intake.

It proves the candidate-facing intake surface only. Real persistence, validation, backend command envelopes, consent ledger, and proof-backed intake records must come later.
