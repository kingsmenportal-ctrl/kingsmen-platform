Application follow-up planner

Record a short, non-sensitive label for each application, its next follow-up date and the evidence status. This planner is stored in this browser only; it does not submit, track or confirm an application with any provider.

Privacy: Do not enter an ID number, password, bank details, private document, or sensitive personal information. This is a local planning aid, not your official application record.
