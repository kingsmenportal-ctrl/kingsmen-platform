Provider connection & partnership readiness

A reliable provider connection starts with verified identity, documented authority, an approved application route and tested controls. A directory listing, technical connection, partnership and live opportunity are different states. None should be inferred from another.

Current public-site boundary: This page is a readiness standard and a device-local checklist. It is not a partner registration form, provider API, integration console or live provider database. Checklist entries are stored in this browser only and are not sent to Kingsmen. Do not enter credentials, API keys, identity numbers, banking details or private candidate information.

What must be established before a provider is connected?

01 · Identity

Legal entity and relationships

Record the registered or legally recognised entity, trading names, aliases, registration or provider identifiers, parent organisation, campuses, branches and delivery locations. Do not collapse separate legal entities into one record.

02 · Authority

Accreditation and scope

Capture the responsible regulator or authority, evidence reference, approved programme or service scope, applicable locations, effective dates, expiry or renewal date and the official source used to verify the claim.

03 · Route

Official application destination

Confirm the provider-controlled application portal, published contact route or documented human-assisted hand-off. An organisation website alone does not prove an application route is operational.

04 · Privacy

Consent and data handling

Agree the lawful purpose, roles, data categories, candidate consent, minimisation, retention, deletion, access controls, incident handling and any required POPIA/data-processing terms before personal information is exchanged.

05 · Integration

Authorised technical access

Use only provider-authorised APIs or access methods. Apply least privilege, secure credential storage, environment separation, audit logs, rate limits, timeouts, idempotency and a tested failure path.

06 · Operations

Evidence and accountability

Define how a route is monitored, who owns exceptions, how stale or withdrawn routes are disabled and what evidence proves a submission was received or a provider made a decision.

Keep provider and opportunity states separate

Provider lifecycle

Directory reference identified

A source or contact record exists. Legal identity is not yet verified.

Legal entity verified

The entity and its aliases/relationships are reconciled against authoritative evidence.

Authority and scope verified

Accreditation, licence or delegated authority is current and covers the recorded service and location.

Provider-authorised route

The official portal, API or human-assisted route has been confirmed with the provider.

Integration connection tested

Permitted test cases, failure handling and audit evidence pass in an approved environment.

Partnership approved

Terms, privacy, operational ownership and required founder approval are recorded.

Opportunity and application evidence

Opportunity recorded

A programme, vacancy or service is linked to a source. It is not automatically open.

Current status checked

Open/closed status, deadline, eligibility, location, costs and source freshness are verified separately.

Candidate authorises assistance

The purpose, information and destination are explained and the necessary consent is captured.

Submission evidence retained

A candidate report is distinct from a provider-confirmed receipt or acknowledgement.

Provider decision recorded

Acceptance, rejection, wait-list or another decision requires provider-origin evidence.

Current opportunity status is separate from provider verification, integration status and partnership approval.

Connection controls that must pass

Security and privacy

  • Documented authority to access the provider system.
  • Separate development, test and production credentials.
  • Secrets held in an approved secret manager, never in browser storage or public code.
  • Minimum necessary candidate data and purpose-limited access.
  • Audit trail for reads, writes, retries, manual interventions and approvals.

Reliability and evidence

  • Test valid and invalid requests, duplicates, timeouts and provider outages.
  • Idempotent handling so retries do not create duplicate applications.
  • Explicit recovery and human hand-off where automation is unavailable.
  • Provider acknowledgement captured separately from a final outcome.
  • Route freshness, ownership and withdrawal procedures documented.
