Use a consistent decision process

Confirm the source

Use the official provider or employer source. Record the date reviewed and re-check time-sensitive information.

Check eligibility and deadlines separately

Programme availability does not establish that you qualify. Confirm requirements and application dates directly with the provider.

Compare the full cost and support

Include fees, travel, accommodation, equipment and living costs. Confirm funding coverage and exclusions in writing.

Consider practical fit and conditions

Assess location, schedule, duration, work conditions, progression, safety and any conditions attached to an offer.

Keep evidence distinct

A candidate-reported submission is not a provider-confirmed receipt. A receipt is not an admission, job offer or funding award.

Storage boundary: This planner saves data in this browser on this device only. It is not synchronised with Kingsmen, does not fetch live listings, verify providers, submit applications or create a recommendation. The authenticated Kingsmen platform must retain candidate decisions server-side with appropriate access controls and audit evidence.
