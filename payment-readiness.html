Payment Safety & Receipt Readiness

Use this checklist to prepare for a legitimate Kingsmen service payment, recognise the difference between a payment attempt and confirmed settlement, and keep the evidence needed to resolve a problem.

Important platform boundary: This public page does not take payments, look up transactions, confirm settlement, issue invoices or activate a service plan. Never submit card details, banking credentials, passwords, PINs or one-time passwords into this checklist.

1. Confirm the purchase

Check the exact plan name, amount in ZAR, inclusions, exclusions, service term, cancellation and refund terms before authorising payment. If the checkout amount differs from the published offer, stop and contact Kingsmen through a verified channel.

2. Use the intended checkout

Start from the official Kingsmen site or an authenticated Kingsmen account when that service is available. Check the domain carefully. Do not follow payment links sent by an unknown person or pay an individual account to secure a placement.

3. Keep proof securely

Retain the official receipt, order reference, amount, currency, date and provider confirmation in a secure location. Redact account numbers and unrelated personal details before sharing evidence with support.

4. Do not assume activation

A checkout page, bank notification or candidate-reported payment is not, by itself, proof that Kingsmen verified settlement. Service entitlements should activate only after the authorised payment confirmation has been validated and recorded.

5. Handle delays safely

Do not pay a second time merely because a page timed out. Save the order reference and receipt, check the official account status when available, and ask support to reconcile the original transaction first.

6. Protect against fraud

Kingsmen will not need your bank password, card PIN or OTP to investigate a receipt. Do not share a full card number or authentication secret. Contact your bank promptly if you suspect unauthorised activity.

If payment is unclear

Resolve before retrying

  1. Keep the order reference and receipt in a secure place.
  2. Record the date, amount and provider status; do not include card or bank secrets.
  3. Check the official account status when the authenticated service is available.
  4. Contact support through independently verified Kingsmen contact details.
  5. Do not submit a duplicate payment unless the original transaction has been reconciled.

Open help & safety guidance

Payment evidence states are different

Checkout started

A payment intent or checkout page exists. It is not evidence that money was received.

Candidate reports payment

A bank alert or candidate statement can help investigation but is not provider-verified settlement.

Provider confirmation validated

The payment provider's server notification, signature, merchant and amount checks have been validated and recorded.

Service entitlement activated

The matching order and entitlement are updated after verified settlement. A payment receipt alone should not promise an outcome or guaranteed placement.

Truth boundary: This is a public, device-local readiness tool. It does not access your Kingsmen account, store transaction evidence on a server, contact PayFast, issue receipts or confirm that a payment is complete. Use only official support channels for real transactions.
