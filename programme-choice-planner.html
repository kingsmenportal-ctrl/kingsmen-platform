Programme choice planner

Organise a single application plan and attach up to six ranked programme choices beneath it. This reflects the Kingsmen parent-application / child-choice structure for planning; it does not create or submit a real CAO or provider application.

Important: Confirm each programme, campus, entry rule, choice limit and deadline on the relevant official provider or admissions service. Ranking an option does not establish eligibility, availability or admission. Never enter an ID number, password, payment credential or private document here.

Evidence rules

  • Planning is not submission.
  • A candidate-reported submission is not provider-confirmed receipt.
  • Provider receipt is not admission or a funding award.
  • Eligibility must be confirmed by the institution or responsible admissions service where required.

How the structure works

One parent: the application authority, cycle, pathway and overall application status.

Child choices: up to six ranked institutions and programmes, each with its own official source and entry-criteria evidence.

The production Kingsmen application service must enforce this structure in the database, authenticate the candidate, preserve immutable audit events and persist provider-confirmed status.

Platform boundary: This is a public browser-local planner, not the authenticated Kingsmen master application or CAO integration. It does not send data to CAO or providers, submit applications, confirm eligibility, reserve places or persist choices on the Kingsmen server.
