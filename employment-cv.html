Employment pathway

Get ready for your next work opportunity

Use this guide to organise a clear CV, prepare for applications and track your own next steps. It does not promise a job or confirm that a vacancy is open.

CV essentials

Keep it relevant and accurate

  • Put your name and reliable contact details at the top of your private CV.
  • Summarise relevant skills and experience in a few clear lines.
  • List work history in reverse chronological order, with dates and measurable responsibilities where possible.
  • Include qualifications, licences and certificates relevant to the role.
  • Check spelling, dates and contact details; save a clean PDF if accepted.
Application safety

Verify before you apply

  • Read the job advert and minimum requirements carefully.
  • Verify the employer and vacancy through an official channel.
  • Tailor your CV truthfully; do not invent qualifications or experience.
  • Never pay a recruiter for a guaranteed job or share passwords, OTPs or banking PINs.
  • Submit documents only through a verified employer or recruitment portal.

After you apply

Keep a private record

Record the employer, role, submission date and reference number in a place you control.

Prepare for the next stage

Review the job description, practise concise examples, and confirm interview arrangements directly with the verified employer.

Verify any offer

Check the written offer and employer identity. Kingsmen does not verify or issue employment offers through this public page.
