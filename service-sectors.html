National service taxonomy

One Kingsmen. More routes through life.

Kingsmen’s discovery model extends beyond education into skills, employment, funding, enterprise, professional development, accommodation, transport, healthcare, legal and social support, and public services.

Important: these 25 entries are service categories, not a live directory of confirmed providers. A category does not prove that a provider is connected, legally accredited, currently available, accepting applications, or that a candidate is eligible. Each exact provider target must be supported by an authoritative source and separately verified before it is presented as active. Kingsmen does not provide clinical or legal professional services.

How categories become verified services

Identify the authoritative source

Record the official provider or government source, responsible authority and retrieval date.

Verify the organisation and scope

Confirm legal identity, accreditation or mandate, delivery scope and relevant locations where applicable.

Verify current service status

Check current availability, eligibility, deadlines, contact channel and official application route. Keep expired, provisional, cancelled and unresolved records distinct.

Prove the connection separately

A public link, partnership, integration, application handoff and provider-confirmed receipt are different evidence states.

This page is a public taxonomy preview. It does not create provider records, perform accreditation checks, submit applications or confirm provider availability.
