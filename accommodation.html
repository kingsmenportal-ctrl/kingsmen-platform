Accommodation pathway

Check housing before you commit

A practical checklist for students, trainees and job seekers comparing accommodation. Kingsmen does not confirm vacancies, reserve rooms or guarantee that a listing is legitimate.

Verify the provider

Check before paying

  • Confirm the property address and inspect it in person or through a trusted representative where feasible.
  • For student residences, confirm any required accreditation or approval directly with the institution or relevant authority.
  • Check who owns or manages the property and ensure the person offering the room is authorised to do so.
  • Get a written lease or agreement, including rent, deposit, utilities, notice periods and refund terms.
  • Do not pay into a personal account solely because someone claims a room is in high demand.
Budget and safety

Calculate the full cost

  • Compare rent plus deposit, transport, utilities, meals, internet and required supplies.
  • Check the route to campus, training site or workplace and the availability of safe transport.
  • Ask about access control, emergency arrangements, maintenance and occupancy rules.
  • Keep receipts and written communication for every payment.
  • Do not send identity documents or banking information to an unverified contact.

Where to verify

Your institution

Ask the institution’s official student housing or financial-aid office about recognised residences, application steps and current arrangements.

Written agreement

Read the full agreement before paying. If unclear, seek independent advice before signing or transferring money.

Report suspected fraud

Preserve messages and payment records. Contact your bank promptly if you suspect a fraudulent transfer and report the matter through appropriate official channels.

Privacy boundary: This public guide does not collect accommodation applications or process payments. Do not treat an advert, directory entry or checklist as proof that a room is available or verified.
