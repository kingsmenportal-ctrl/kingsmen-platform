Funding checklist

Confirm the qualification

Check the institution, programme name, accreditation and admission requirements first.

Check eligibility and dates

Read the current rules from the funder. Do not rely on old social posts or unofficial messages.

Prepare evidence

The funder may request identity, household-income or academic evidence. Upload sensitive documents only to the funder’s verified secure portal.

Keep proof and follow up

Save the official reference number and confirmation. A submitted application is not proof of approval or payment.

Scam warning: Never pay a person who promises guaranteed NSFAS approval, a bursary or a job. Verify contact details through the official organisation and never share one-time PINs or passwords.
