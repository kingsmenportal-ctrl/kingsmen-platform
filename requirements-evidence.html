Requirements & evidence planner

Record each requirement from an official institution, training provider, funder or employer source. Track what you have checked and prepared while keeping your own report distinct from provider-confirmed receipt or acceptance.

Accuracy and privacy: Confirm every requirement, deadline, fee and accepted document format using the official provider source. A checklist entry is not proof of eligibility or acceptance. Do not enter identity numbers, passwords, bank details, medical information or private document contents. This planner does not upload files.

Evidence states mean different things

Official requirement confirmed

The requirement appears on an official source checked on the recorded date. It does not confirm that you meet it.

Candidate-reported ready or submitted

This is your own report. It is not provider verification, receipt or acceptance.

Provider-confirmed received or waived

Use only when official communication or the provider portal supports that exact state.

Use the right authority

  • Eligibility and entry criteria come from the responsible institution or programme rules.
  • Application fees and payment instructions must be checked on the official provider channel.
  • Document formats, certification rules and validity periods can differ by provider.
  • Only the provider can confirm receipt, acceptance, waiver or a final outcome.
Platform boundary: This is a public, browser-local planning utility. It is not Kingsmen's authenticated application workflow or Document Vault. It does not assess eligibility, validate certificates, submit applications, receive provider data or update a candidate profile.
