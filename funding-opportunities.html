OPEN · National · Teaching qualification

Funza Lushaka Bursary Programme · 2027

New applicants close: 31 January 2027. Returning bursars close: 30 November 2026.

Who may qualify: First-time applicants must be 30 or younger, have passed matric with a Bachelor's pass or meet university entrance requirements, and pursue an eligible teaching qualification. Priority fields include STEM, South African indigenous languages and inclusive education, aligned to provincial subject needs.

Support: The DBE describes support for tuition, accommodation or transport, meals, learning materials, personal care and teaching practice. Conditions and award amounts must be checked in the current application process.

Apply: Applications use the government e-Services portal. Read the DBE programme notice and use the official portal only.

OPEN · Gauteng · University and TVET

City of Ekurhuleni Community Bursary · 2027

Closing: 30 November 2026. Applications are open according to the City notice published 30 October 2026.

Who may qualify: South African citizens permanently resident in Ekurhuleni; age 18–35; completed Grade 12; accepted or registered for 2027 study; annual household income not exceeding R600,000. Study must be full-time in a listed field at an accredited South African university, university of technology or TVET college.

Listed fields: Built environment; ICT including AI and Internet of Things; finance; environmental health; and specified TVET technical, artisan and apprenticeship qualifications (including N3 entry for the stated TVET group).

Documents: Certified ID, acceptance or registration evidence, Ekurhuleni proof of residence and household-income evidence. The City requires certification no older than three months.

Apply: Create a profile, verify the email, select Community Bursary and submit online. Retain the generated reference number. A valid email is required for acknowledgement and outcome communication.

OPEN · North West · Provincial bursary

Victor Thebe Sifora Provincial Bursary Scheme · 2027

Closing: 13 November 2026 at 23:59 SAST. The official portal reports the current intake open from 13 October 2026.

Who may qualify: Applicants should verify the active intake's residency, study-level and field conditions directly with the North West Provincial Government. The portal warns that current intake requirements may differ from the previous advert; do not rely on an older copy.

Evidence listed on the portal: Completed bursary application form, proof of permanent residential address, certified student ID, matric certificate / results as applicable, latest tertiary results for current students, and parent or guardian income evidence.

Apply: Use the official provincial bursary portal and confirm the current programme conditions before uploading documents.

Before submitting a bursary application

Check personal eligibility

Confirm citizenship, residency, age, household-income threshold, academic standing and accepted field with the funder.

Check the exact qualification and institution

A bursary may cover only named fields, institutions or full-time study. Do not assume every programme is covered.

Prepare certified evidence

Follow the funder's certification age, file format and document instructions. Upload identity and income records only to the verified official portal.

Keep provider evidence

Save the official reference number and acknowledgement. Distinguish a candidate-reported submission from a provider-confirmed receipt and from an award decision.

Fraud prevention: Do not pay an intermediary for access, guaranteed placement or guaranteed funding. Never share a password or OTP. Verify every application link from the funder's official website.
