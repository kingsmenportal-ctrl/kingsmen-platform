SETA Funding & Workplace Learning Windows

A dated watchlist of Services SETA 2026/27 grant windows that can support workplace learning, skills programmes, learnerships and apprenticeships.

Checked 1 November 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg): The three windows below are official Services SETA funding opportunities for organisations and public institutions—not personal bursaries for individual learners. Their common published closing date is 6 November 2026. The official pages do not state a closing time in the information checked here; do not assume one. Reopen the official notices before preparing or submitting a proposal.

OPEN AT SNAPSHOT · Employers / accredited providers

Discretionary Grant Funding Window 2026/27 · Reference 2026/27-DG-03

Closing date: 6 November 2026. The official page does not specify a closing time in the information checked.

Eligible applicants: Employers, sectoral associations, professional bodies, trade unions and federations, and accredited Skills Development Providers within Services SETA's scope.

Programmes supported: Learnerships, apprenticeships, Recognition of Prior Learning (including Artisan RPL), Adult Education and Training, skills programmes and candidacy. Eligibility varies by programme; proposals must align with the SETA's sector priorities and hard-to-fill vacancies. The official notice says a maximum of two learning programmes may be applied for.

Submission: Review the advert, Annexure A and the current application instructions on Services SETA's official page. A grant award is not guaranteed and depends on evaluation, verification, budget and approval.

OPEN AT SNAPSHOT · Registered non-profit organisations

Strategic Projects Funding Window 2026/27 · Reference SP2026/27-04

Closing date: 6 November 2026. The official page does not specify a closing time in the information checked.

Eligible applicants: Legally registered South African NGOs, NPOs, NPCs, community-based organisations and community trusts.

Focus areas: Rural and township development, youth and women empowerment, disability inclusion, support for military veterans and dependants, enterprise development, self-employment, job creation and local economic development.

Submission status: The official notice describes the required proposal, project plan, budget and declarations, but says details of the application platform and submission process will be published on the page. Confirm the currently available submission route directly with Services SETA before sending documentation.

OPEN AT SNAPSHOT · Public institutions

Strategic Partnership Funding Window 2026/27 · Public entities

Closing date: 6 November 2026. The official page does not specify a closing time in the information checked.

Eligible applicants: Public institutions including national and provincial government, municipalities, TVET colleges, CET colleges and public entities across the three spheres of government.

Focus areas: Rural and township development, youth and women empowerment, enterprise development, self-employment, job creation and local economic development, with projects tied to measurable outcomes and service-delivery needs.

Submission status: The official notice says application-platform and submission-process details will be published on the notice page. Confirm the exact process, templates, declarations and eligibility before relying on a submission route.
