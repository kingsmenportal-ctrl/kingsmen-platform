Verify before you act

Opportunity verification & scam safety

A directory entry is a starting point, not proof that a programme is open, that you qualify, or that an application was received. Verify each material claim at its source.

Candidate safety

Stop. Check. Confirm.

Never pay an individual to “secure” a place or share passwords, one-time pins, or banking credentials.

Verification standard

What must be checked?

1. Source authenticity

Reach the institution, employer, SETA, funder or government body through its independently located official website. Check the domain and contact details; do not trust a forwarded link alone.

2. Open status & deadline

Confirm the specific intake or vacancy is currently open, its closing date and time, and any late-application rules. An old advert or active website does not prove an opportunity is open.

3. Eligibility & evidence

Read the exact age, citizenship/residency, qualification, subject, location and supporting-document criteria. If unclear, ask the provider through an official contact channel.

4. Costs & payment

Confirm fees, deposits, refund terms and the payee using official published details. Do not send money to a personal account, buy a guaranteed placement, or pay for an interview.

5. Submission proof

Keep the confirmation email, reference number, receipt or dated screenshot. A candidate's report that they submitted is not the same as provider-confirmed receipt or an outcome.

6. Privacy & impersonation

Share only required documents through a verified channel. Never disclose passwords or OTPs. Be suspicious of urgency, guaranteed selection, spelling-heavy messages and requests to move to private messaging.

Report a concern

If something looks wrong

  1. Stop the payment or document transfer.
  2. Preserve messages, URLs, receipts and reference numbers.
  3. Contact the genuine provider using contact details found independently.
  4. If money or identity information was exposed, contact your bank and the relevant authorities promptly.
