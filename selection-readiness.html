Selection & assessment readiness

Organise preparation for interviews, entrance assessments, practical demonstrations, portfolio reviews and other selection stages. Use one plan per opportunity and keep provider-confirmed events separate from your own reports.

Safety and privacy: Confirm every invitation and venue through the institution, funder or employer's official channel. Never pay for a guaranteed interview, selection result or job. Do not store identity numbers, passwords, OTPs, bank details, private documents or sensitive personal information in this public tool.

Prepare for the stage, not just the appointment

Understand the criteria

Use the official programme or job advert to identify what will be assessed. Do not rely on rumours or paid intermediaries.

Prepare evidence-based examples

Practise explaining what you did, the skills used and the result. Keep claims accurate and consistent with your actual experience.

Check practical arrangements

Confirm venue, link, time zone, permitted materials, equipment, travel and accessibility needs with the official contact.

Record the outcome carefully

Your attendance report is not a provider result. Record selection or rejection only when the responsible organisation communicates it.
