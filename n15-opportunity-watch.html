What this page does not claim

  • It is not the live authenticated Kingsmen N15 candidate feed and is not synchronised with the staging database.
  • No candidate-specific eligibility, ranking, recommendation or match has been calculated.
  • No opportunity application has been submitted, and no provider acknowledgement, receipt or outcome is recorded.
  • Listings from higher education, TVET, SETA, learnership, internship and apprenticeship pathways are not shown here unless their current official evidence has been rechecked.

Do not pay a recruiter or intermediary to access a government vacancy. Follow the official advert and use only the application route published by the responsible authority.

How Kingsmen treats evidence

A source directory is not an open opportunity. A dated official listing is a source observation, not a candidate match. Candidate-reported submission, provider-confirmed receipt and provider decision remain separate evidence states.
