Get application-ready

A practical application checklist

Different providers ask for different things. Use this checklist to prepare, then follow the exact instructions on the official application portal.

Choose the right opportunity

Check the qualification or job, provider, location, eligibility, closing date and whether the opportunity is still open.

Read requirements before starting

List every required field and document. Check file formats, file-size limits, certified-copy rules and any application fee directly with the provider.

Prepare your documents securely

Common requests may include an ID document, school results or qualifications, CV, proof of address and supporting certificates. Only prepare what the provider requests.

Complete the official form

Use the official provider portal. Check spelling, contact details and programme choices. Never give your password or OTP to another person.

Submit and save proof

Save the confirmation page, email and reference number. A draft or completed form is not necessarily submitted.

Track and verify the outcome

Use the provider’s official portal or contact channel to check progress. Treat an offer, rejection or funding decision as real only when confirmed by the provider.

Personal preparation checklist

Tick items on this device as you prepare. This checklist stays in this browser page only; it is not saved to a Kingsmen account or sent to a server.
