Candidate communications · N09 readiness

Communicate safely. Keep evidence clear.

A reliable application journey needs a trusted communication channel, careful handling of personal information and accurate records of what a candidate sent versus what a provider actually received or decided.

Current channel boundary: Email is the primary Kingsmen communication channel. WhatsApp and SMS are not enabled by this public site. This page is a safety and preparation guide, not a connected inbox, email sender, reminder service or support-ticket system. Do not enter private messages or personal information here.

Before trusting a message

1 · Sender

Check the real sender address

Read the full email address, not only the display name. Compare its domain with the provider contact details published on the provider’s official website.

2 · Destination

Inspect the official provider domain

Do not use shortened or unexpected links to sign in, upload documents or pay. Navigate to the official provider website independently and locate the same application or notice.

3 · Request

Challenge unusual urgency

Guaranteed placement, guaranteed funding, threats of immediate cancellation and pressure to pay a private person are warning signs. Confirm unusual requests with the provider through independently sourced contact details.

4 · Information

Protect sensitive information

Never share an OTP, password, full card details, banking PIN or account recovery code. Do not send an identity document or bank statement to an unverified address.

Use one evidence vocabulary

Candidate activity

  • Draft prepared: saved for the candidate; nothing has been sent.
  • Candidate-reported sent: the candidate says a message or application was sent; this is not provider confirmation.
  • Candidate evidence captured: the candidate retains a timestamp, sent-mail copy or reference number.

Provider-side evidence

  • Provider-confirmed receipt: an acknowledgement originating from the provider or its authorised portal has been verified.
  • Provider decision: an official admission, funding, interview, rejection or employment decision is recorded with evidence.
  • Human review: Kingsmen staff review evidence and any correction or escalation; AI/Jarvis may assist but cannot approve or invent a provider response.

An email in a candidate’s Sent folder can support a candidate-reported event. It does not, by itself, prove delivery, provider receipt, acceptance or an outcome.

What to retain when following up

Safe message evidence

Keep the date and time (with timezone), sender and recipient domains, subject line, provider reference number, the official portal route and a copy of the message in the candidate’s own secure account.

Documents and attachments

Only send documents requested by the verified provider through its official secure channel. Check the recipient and attachment before sending. Keep the submitted version and the provider’s receipt separately.

Follow-up discipline

Use the provider’s stated response period. Quote the existing reference, ask one clear question and avoid opening duplicate applications. Escalate through a published provider contact or an authorised Kingsmen support route.
